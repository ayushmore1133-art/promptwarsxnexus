Civic → Mobility → Pollution Feedback Loop

How citizen-reported road problems continuously feed into Chennai transit intelligence.

1
Citizen Reports Road Hazard

Commuter uploads photo and geolocates pothole/flooding on the map (Status: 🔴 Not Started).

2
Spatial Corridor Ingestion

VAAYU corridor auditor detects the hazard on road segments and alerts Sentinel Agent.

3
Commuters Warned & Rerouted

Private vehicle safety is penalized; smart public transit is promoted to reduce road congestion.

4
Repair & Complainer Audit

GCC completes repair (🟤 Pending ➔ ✅ Solved); complainer verifies with green tick to clear hazard.

📐 Scoring Model & Assumptions: VAAYU Score weights: Travel Time (20%), CO₂ Emissions (25%), Air Pollution Exposure (20%), Road Safety (20%), Trip Cost (15%). Emission factors: Car (180g/km), Two-wheeler (72g/km), Bus+Metro (28g/pkm), EV (50g/km), Bicycle/Walk (0g/km). CO₂ values are estimates based on configurable average emission factors.
